# SCRIPT — iom-mock-tiktok

**Voice:** af_sky (Kokoro, local) — young, direct female; the same age as the
student on screen, so the narration reads as her thought rather than an
announcer's.
**Voice settings:** provider default (Kokoro exposes no stability/style knobs)
**Voice direction:** Fast and level, not salesy. The first three lines are said
flat, the way you state something obvious that nobody says out loud. Lift only
on the last two lines. Never sell the numbers — let them sit.

---

## Line 1 — Hook (Frame 1)

**Time:** 0.0 – 4.1s
**Delivery:** Flat, certain, no lift at the end. A statement of fact. The
letters of MECEE-BL are said individually — "em ee si ee ee bee el" — not run
together. Kokoro reads the literal string "MECEE-BL" as the word "messy BL"
(confirmed by transcribing the take), so the exam's name is respelled
phonetically below for the synthesiser only. The burned caption still reads
MECEE-BL.

    Nobody fails em ee si ee ee bee el on the questions they don't know.

## Line 2 — The pain (Frame 2)

**Time:** 4.0 – 8.0s
**Delivery:** Land "guessed" and stop. The counter and the bites carry the rest of the frame.

    They fail on the ones they guessed.

## Line 3 — The agitation (Frame 3)

**Time:** 8.0 – 12.3s
**Delivery:** Three separate hits. A full beat between each — this is the arithmetic landing.

    Thirty-eight guesses. Nine and a half marks gone. About a thousand ranks.

## Line 4 — The turn (Frame 4)

**Time:** 12.3 – 16.1s
**Delivery:** The first warmth in the read. "Stop guessing" is an instruction, not a scold.

    So stop guessing. Every wrong answer comes back worked out.

## Line 5 — Product intro (Frame 5)

**Time:** 16.1 – 19.9s
**Delivery:** Clipped and factual. Three specs, three beats — credibility comes from the plainness.

    Two hundred questions. Three hours. Marked like the real paper.

## Line 6 — The payoff (Frame 6)

**Time:** 19.9 – 23.4s
**Delivery:** Quieter and slower than everything before it. This is the line the video was built for.

    Then it shows you where you actually stand.

## Line 7 — CTA (Frame 7)

**Time:** 23.4 – 26.6s
**Delivery:** Light, close to a shrug. "Tonight" is the word that has to land.

    Two full mocks, free. Sit one tonight.

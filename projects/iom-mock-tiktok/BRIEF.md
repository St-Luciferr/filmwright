## Intent

A TikTok promo for **IOM Mock Entrance**, an independent MECEE-BL (MBBS/BDS)
mock-test platform for Nepal. The chosen concept is **"−0.25"**: negative
marking is personified as a small gremlin that follows a student around and
bites a quarter-mark off her score every time she guesses. It grows fat while
she guesses blind; the app starves it once she can see her per-question pace,
worked solutions, and where she actually ranks.

The point of the character is that the video must not be text-and-lines motion
graphics — the user asked explicitly for a character. Two of them: the student
(protagonist) and the gremlin (antagonist). Tone: fast, funny-anxious, then
decisive. It must stop a thumb in the first 0.6s and stay legible muted.

## Assets

- Captured screens from the running app at http://localhost:3000 — hero moment is
  the dashboard **exam-readiness / rank card**, the one screen that stays legible
  punched in at phone size.
- Brand tokens from `tailwind.config.ts`: teal `#0c7c7c` (brand-600), ink
  `#393f49`, ink/2 `#4f5a65`.

## Customizations

- **Two-character scene** — student protagonist plus the "−0.25" gremlin antagonist.
  The gremlin is the recurring visual motif; it must appear in the first second.
- **Score counter ticking down** in the corner while the gremlin feeds, then
  recovering once the app is introduced.
- **SFX on every bite** — the gremlin's chomps are the rhythmic spine of the cut.
- **Voiceover + music bed + burned-in captions**, all three. Music ducked under
  the VO (voiceover carve) so the narration stays clear.
- Product shown as **both**: real captured screens for the hero rank moment,
  recreated vector UI (brand teal) for the fast beats.
